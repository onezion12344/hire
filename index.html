Questions that come up

short answers

Can you work with Google Sheets instead of Excel?

Yes. Share a copy or export a CSV. The deliverable is a file you can open again, plus the steps to rerun it — not a live connection into your account.

What if my file is bigger than the limit?

Send it. I quote the difference before starting, and the acceptance test is rewritten to match the real size. Nothing is charged before you say go.

How is this different from a cheap gig?

You get a written exception log and a validation report, not just an output file. If a row was dropped, you can see which one and why. That is most of the work.

Do you keep my data?

No. It is worked on a copy, deleted after handover, and never appears in the portfolio. The README shows you how to run the same thing again on your own machine.

Can you start today?

Usually the next working day. I take one job at a time and two a week at most, so the first reply includes a real start date rather than “as soon as possible”.

What can't you do?

No medical, legal or financial advice; no written-off promises about hours saved; no reviews I did not earn; nothing that needs me to hold your account passwords or auto-send messages in your name.
